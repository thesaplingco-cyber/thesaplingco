About this plant

With multiple stalks of the plant carefully arranged to form pyramids of different shapes and sizes, the Lucky Bamboo Pyramid is a wonderful, super lucky, and very aesthetically pleasing plant to have indoors. Since the stalks of the plant are held together by decorative wire ties, the aesthetic appeal of the plant is greatly enhanced. Moreover, the Lucky Bamboo plant is one of the best Feng Shui plants to keep at home, making it whether you’re looking to bring it home or gift it to your loved ones.

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
