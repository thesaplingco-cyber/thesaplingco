About this plant

A beloved indoor plant around the world, the Money Plant Golden is every gardener’s favorite and with very good reason! Highly adaptable, versatile, low maintenance, and timeless, the Money Plant Gold en graces almost every garden, everywhere. This plant can survive some periods of neglect and continue to grace your gardens with an evergreen elegance. Its growth patterns also allow it to grow well as a cascading vine or a creeping plant when trained.

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
