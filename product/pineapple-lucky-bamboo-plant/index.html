About this plant

Introducing the Pineapple Lucky Bamboo Plant, a unique and captivating arrangement that brings an exotic touch of nature into your home or office. This distinctive bamboo formation, resembling the shape of a pineapple, symbolizes good luck, prosperity, and harmony. Its vibrant green stems and leaves are a testament to its health and vitality, making it a perfect centerpiece for any space. Whether as a decorative piece or a thoughtful gift, this Lucky Bamboo Plant is sure to uplift the ambiance with its elegance and positive energy.

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to outdoor / terrace placement.
