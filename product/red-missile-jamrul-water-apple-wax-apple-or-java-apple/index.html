About this plant

Red missile Jamrul (water apple, wax apple, or Java apple) (Syzygium samarangense) is a exotic & rare fruit plant suited to Outdoor / Terrace growing. Best placed in Full Sun and watered Moderate. Maintenance: Medium. Container suitable: Large-container / ground.

How to care for it

Loves bright, direct sun — ideal for a sunny balcony or terrace.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to outdoor / terrace placement.
