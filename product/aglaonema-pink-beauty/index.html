About this plant

Aglaonema Pink Beauty (Aglaonema 'Pink Beauty') is a popular home-garden plant suited to aglaonema. Best placed in low to bright indirect and watered low–moderate. Maintenance: low. Container suitable: yes.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor placement.
