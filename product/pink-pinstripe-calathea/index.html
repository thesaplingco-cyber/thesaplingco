About this plant

Pink Pinstripe Calathea (Calathea ornata) is a popular home-garden plant suited to calathea. Best placed in bright indirect / low light and watered high. Maintenance: medium. Container suitable: large-container / ground.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
2–3× a week — Likes lightly moist soil — don't let it dry out fully.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to indoor placement.
