About this plant

Elevate your indoor space with the Aglaonema Snow White plant. With its striking variegated leaves and low-maintenance nature, it's the perfect addition to any home. Thriving in moderate light and adaptable to various conditions, it's a breeze to care for, even for beginners. Ideal for all décor themes, the Aglaonema Snow White brings a touch of nature's elegance indoors. Its lush green and creamy white foliage complements both sleek modern spaces and culturally rich traditional interiors. This plant not only purifies the air but also adds a sense of tranquillity to your surroundings. Place it in living rooms, bedrooms, or offices, its charm and adaptability make it a must-have indoor companion for all décor styles.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
