About this plant

As one of the most popular and renowned Feng Shui plants in the world, the Lotus Lucky Bamboo makes for the perfect indoor plants - whether you’re looking to gift it or keep it in your home or office space. The five thick stalks of the plant symbolize balance and peace, often being respected as harbingers of luck and positivity into the lives of their caretakers. This stunning indoor plant boasts unique stems and lovely patterns with a dense foliage that greatly adds to its charm. Additionally, this plant is super low maintenance and fuss-free, making it perfect for new and experienced gardeners alike.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
