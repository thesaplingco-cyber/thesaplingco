About this plant

If you are a first time gardener looking for an easy one, Aglaonema Pink Beauty is the plant for you. Also called the Chinese Evergreen, it will stay happy and beautiful in low indirect light with little water. Its bright pink leaves add the right amount of jazz and colour to any space and décor. Its a stunner with its pretty pink foliage both alone and in plant clusters.

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
