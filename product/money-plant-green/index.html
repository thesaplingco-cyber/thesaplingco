About this plant

Timeless, classy, and a zero-fuss stunner, the Green Money Plant is one of those plants every plant parent just must have! This fast-growing tropical is an excellent first-time plant for newbies and pros alike. It does not demand a lot of care, allowing it to fit in perfectly with your busy schedules, travel plans, and more. Simply place it near a brightly lit window or let it trail delicately from hanging baskets, and watch it light up your space with its cozy foliage and easy-on-the-eye aesthetics!

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
