About this plant

Dark foliage that stands out, effortlessly The Philodendron Black Cardinal is all about depth and contrast. Its rich, burgundy-black leaves bring a bold, slightly dramatic feel to your space, the kind that instantly makes a room look more put together. Compact and naturally full, it works well on side tables, shelves, or corners that need a stronger visual anchor. Why it works • Deep, dark leaves that add instant contrast • Compact, bushy growth that looks full from day one • Elevates living rooms, bedrooms and workspaces • Pairs beautifully with lighter green plants Easy to live with Despite its premium look, it’s easy to care for. Place it in bright, indirect light and water when the top layer of soil feels dry, it adapts well to indoor conditions and grows steadily.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
