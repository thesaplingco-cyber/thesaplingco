About this plant

Low maintenance, perfectly variegated, and a great air purifier, the Variegated Peperomia plant has it all! Its succulent-like leaves mean you don’t have to water it too often and its stunning aesthetics allow you to proudly display it all throughout your house. Talk about versatile and easy to maintain - the Peperomia obtusifolia variegated is the perfect indoor plant. Place it on desktops or grow it as a trailing vine, this semi-succulent just won’t let you down!

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
