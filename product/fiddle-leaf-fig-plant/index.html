About this plant

The coolest kid on the block at the moment and an Instagram superstar, the Ficus Lyrata or the Fiddle Leaf Fig is a tropical plant that is also a great air purifier. Give it a spot with enough light and proper watering and it will gift you with beautiful broad leaves that will make your space worthy of a magazine feature.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to outdoor / terrace placement.
