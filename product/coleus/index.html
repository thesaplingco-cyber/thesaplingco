About this plant

Coleus (Plectranthus scutellarioides) is a popular home-garden plant suited to coleus. Best placed in bright indirect / morning sun and watered moderate. Maintenance: low. Container suitable: large-container / ground.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor shade placement.
