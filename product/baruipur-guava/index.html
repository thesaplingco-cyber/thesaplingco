About this plant

Baruipur Guava (Psidium guajava) is a popular home-garden plant suited to fruit garden. Best placed in full sun and watered moderate. Maintenance: low. Container suitable: yes.

How to care for it

Loves bright, direct sun — ideal for a sunny balcony or terrace.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to outdoor / terrace placement.
