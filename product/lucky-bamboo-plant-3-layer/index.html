About this plant

Easy to grow and care for, the Lucky Bamboo plant is a popular indoor plant because of more reasons than just its care requirements. According to Feng Shui, this is one of the best lucky plants for homes, and its reputation as such greatly adds to its charm and allure. Additionally, it is also a great gifting option that works as a personal pick-me-up!

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
