About this plant

This plant looks great when planted indoors. It features large, leathery, arrow-shaped green leaves. Its unusual, large, heart-shaped, showy red flowers bloom indoors. It is high on maintenance can be planted in decorative pots in the living room.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
