About this plant

Tall, slender, and majestic - the Money Tree is known for its beautiful foliage and braided stems. Beloved for being low-maintenance and fuss-free, the Money Tree is also a popularly known and gifted token of good fortune. Scientifically known as the Pachira Aquatica, the Money Tree can easily take the centerstage in your home decor and effortlessly grace any space with its stunning aesthetics.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
