About this plant

Dracaena Lemon Lime brightens your space with bold, lime-yellow stripes on glossy green leaves. Sleek, modern, and refreshingly easy to care for, it thrives in low to medium light and purifies the air as it grows. Whether placed on a shelf or in a forgotten corner, it instantly lifts the mood and the decor.

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to outdoor / terrace placement.
