About this plant

Croton Plants (Short Leaves, thick leaves) (Codiaeum variegatum) is a popular home-garden plant suited to croton. Best placed in bright indirect / morning sun and watered moderate. Maintenance: medium. Container suitable: large-container / ground.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to indoor / outdoor shade placement.
