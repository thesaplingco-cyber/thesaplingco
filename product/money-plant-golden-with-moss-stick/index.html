About this plant

The Money Plant Golden is a timeless houseplant that can be found in almost every household worldwide. Whether you're a new gardener or a seasoned expert, this stunning plant will thrive under your care. It does not demand too much of your time or attention and does well with just a little bit of the right kind of care. Keep your plant in bright, indirect sunlight; give it water twice a week; and watch it thrive! The best part is that this stunner grows wonderfully on a moss stick due to its fast growth and beautiful climbing pattern, making it ideal for every setting and all kinds of home decor.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
