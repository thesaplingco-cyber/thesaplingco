About this plant

If you have ever seen, cared for, or received as a gift the Heart Hoya, or Hoya Kerrii plant, then you already know that she is also called the Valentine plant and the Sweetheart Wax plant not only because she is shaped like a heart, but also because she touches the heart. Every plant is a little 5 inch heart shaped leaf and when placed in a basket, eventually they grow into a bushy bunch of green hearts.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
