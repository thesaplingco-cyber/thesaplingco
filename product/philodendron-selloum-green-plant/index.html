About this plant

Also known as the Tree Philodendron, the Philodendron Selloum Plant is an XL plant known for its large, glossy leaves and ease of care. This stunning tropical plant prefers a humid indoor environment and with the right care, it can stay with you for more than a decade! So, whether you’re looking for the perfect living room centerpiece or the last missing piece for your bedroom’s decor, the Philodendron Selloum is the perfect addition to your home!

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
