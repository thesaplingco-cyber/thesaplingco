About this plant

The Green Peperomia isn’t just a pretty face! It’s a low-maintenance air purifier that brightens your space while removing toxins. Perfect for homes and offices, this pet-friendly plant adds a fresh, calming vibe. A big bonus? Its compact size makes it perfect for small spaces and classy indoor plant displays!

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
