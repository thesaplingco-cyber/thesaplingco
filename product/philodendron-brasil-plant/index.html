About this plant

Looking for an indoor plant with perfect patterns on delicate leaves that don’t need too much maintenance? Then the Philodendron Brasil Plant is the perfect fit for you and your home! Beautiful green-yellow variegations adorn this plant that thrives when placed in bright, indirect light and given water just twice a week. Brighten up any corner of your home with this stunning Philodendron.

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
