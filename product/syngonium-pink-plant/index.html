About this plant

The Pink Syngonium Plant is absolutely just the cutest ornamental creeper you will have ever met. Its adorable pink arrow-shaped leaves make it look so perfect that it feels handmade. Ideal for both tabletops and floor planters, with proper care, this stunning indoor plant can grow up to 3 to 6 feet. Also known as the Goosefoot or Pink Arrowhead plant , it is wonderfully unpredictable and versatile because as a baby it grows upright but can either trail or climb as it grows older. With this plant, the decor canvas is all yours, paint away!

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
