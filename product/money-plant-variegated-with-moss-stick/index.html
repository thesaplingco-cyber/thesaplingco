About this plant

There is nothing as easy and as fast to grow as a money plant and any one, gardener or not, can attest to it. The money plant variegated is an easy to grow tropical plant variety with heart shaped leaves in beautiful green colour with stunning variegations. The money plant is an excellent air purifiers and grow happily in indoor conditions to improve oxygen saturation in indoor air. While the plant can both trail and climb, a money plant variegated growing on a moss stick looks beautiful. The moss stick allows the money plant to grow in a compact manner and grow vertically to add height to your garden space. The moss stick is made of sphagnum moss that is excellent for plant growth. It retains the right amount of moisture allowing the aerial roots to get nutrition and also prevents the air from drying out.

How to care for it

Bright, indirect light near a well-lit window; avoid harsh midday sun.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
