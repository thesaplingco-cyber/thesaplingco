About this plant

Add a zesty twist to your garden with the classic Lemon Plant! This sun-loving beauty thrives in warm climates and rewards you with juicy, vitamin C-packed lemons bursting with tangy freshness. Perfect for both gardens and large pots, it loves well-drained soil and some extra love. With a little effort and lots of love, you’ll have homegrown lemons ready to squeeze, slice, and zest—straight from your tree to your kitchen!

How to care for it

Loves bright, direct sun — ideal for a sunny balcony or terrace.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Easy-going with a light routine; the main risk is overwatering.
Suited to outdoor / terrace placement.
