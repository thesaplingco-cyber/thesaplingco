About this plant

After its grand debut on the global gardening scene in 2019, the Black ZZ is at the very top of the list for many plant parents. A variant of the popular Green ZZ, this plant has the darkest purple leaves that almost look black, and is one of the easiest plants to care for. It can thrive in low light conditions and prefers minimal watering, allowing it to thrive in gardens everywhere without much care. This makes the Zamia ZZ Black Plant perfect for new and experienced gardeners alike!

How to care for it

Tolerates low light — good for shaded corners and offices.
Every 10–14 days — Drought-tolerant — let the soil dry out between waterings.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to indoor / outdoor placement.
