About this plant

Variegated Morpankhi (Oriental Arborvitae, chinese thuja) (Platycladus orientalis) is a popular home-garden plant suited to morpankhi. Best placed in full sun / bright light and watered moderate. Maintenance: low. Container suitable: large-container / ground.

How to care for it

Loves bright, direct sun — ideal for a sunny balcony or terrace.
About once a week — Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry.
Beginner-friendly and hard to kill — main pitfall is overwatering, so let it dry between drinks.
Suited to outdoor placement.
