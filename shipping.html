How we pack

Live plants are delicate, so we don't cut corners. Every plant is:

  • Secured at the root with moist coco peat to retain moisture in transit
  • Wrapped in newspaper cushioning to protect leaves and stems
  • Boxed in a sturdy corrugated carton, clearly marked as a live plant

When your order ships

You'll receive a confirmation with tracking details once your plant is on its way. You can also reach us anytime on WhatsApp for an update.

If your plant arrives damaged

We pack carefully, but transit can occasionally be rough. If your plant arrives damaged, message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the plant and packaging, and we'll make it right. Please keep the original packaging until the issue is resolved.

Delivery areas

We currently ship across India. Some live plants may be restricted from certain regions due to agricultural or courier rules — if that affects your order, we'll contact you.
